What loan protection cover does

Loan protection is designed for borrowers with an eligible loan. In the covered event described in the policy, the benefit helps settle the outstanding loan balance, which can reduce the debt left to repay.

The amount payable is limited by the outstanding balance and the policy terms. Check the insurer's documents for eligibility, exclusions, limits and other conditions before applying.
