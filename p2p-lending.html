Cover for peer-to-peer loans

Peer-to-peer (P2P) lending is a loan made directly between individuals. This eDIP option is designed to help protect the lender's unpaid loan balance if the insured borrower dies during the active cover period.

The benefit is limited by the outstanding balance and the policy terms. Review eligibility, exclusions, limits and conditions in the insurer's documents before applying.
