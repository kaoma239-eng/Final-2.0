Loan Protection Coverage

Choose the cover that matches your loan arrangement. Each option protects an outstanding loan or advance balance for a selected period of 1 to 12 months, subject to the insurer's policy terms.

Loan Protection

For a qualifying loan. If the insured borrower dies while cover is active, the benefit helps pay the outstanding loan balance, reducing the debt left to repay. The benefit is limited by the balance and policy terms.

  • For borrowers with an eligible loan
  • Helps settle the unpaid loan balance
  • Choose a cover period from 1 to 12 months
Calculate Premium

Salary Advance

For an advance on an employee's salary from their employer. If the insured employee dies while cover is active, the benefit helps clear the unpaid advance balance, subject to the policy terms.

  • Designed for employer-provided salary advances
  • Helps reduce the employer's outstanding advance
  • Choose a cover period from 1 to 12 months
Calculate Premium

P2P Lending

For a loan made directly between individuals. If the insured borrower dies while cover is active, the benefit helps repay the lender for the outstanding balance, subject to the policy terms.

  • Designed for peer-to-peer loans
  • Helps protect the lender's unpaid loan balance
  • Choose a cover period from 1 to 12 months
